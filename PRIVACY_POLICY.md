# Privacy Policy

**Bottle Sort Puzzle**
Last updated: April 29, 2026

## Overview

Bottle Sort Puzzle is an offline puzzle game. We do not collect, store, or share any personal information.

## Data We Collect

We collect no personal data whatsoever. The app does not require an account, does not connect to the internet, and does not communicate with any external server.

## Data Stored on Your Device

The app saves your game progress (completed levels) locally on your device using on-device storage. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

## Third-Party Services

The app does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or tracking services.

## Permissions

The app does not request any special device permissions (camera, microphone, location, contacts, etc.).

## Children's Privacy

The app is suitable for all ages. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, no special handling for children's data is required.

## Changes to This Policy

If we ever update this policy, the new version will be posted here with an updated date.
